## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>What is Snap Translate?</b></summary>
<br>

Snap Translate is translation software for Windows from LifeAi that translates what's on your screen and your documents. In screen translation, the translation appears exactly in place of the original text. In document translation, PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files are translated with their original formatting.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is Snap Translate free?</b></summary>
<br>

Yes. Snap Translate is free for personal, non-commercial use. For commercial use, for example in a business or an organization, <a href="https://lifeai.ovh/en/contact">contact us</a> for a license. The full terms are in the license agreement that comes with the software.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I translate a PDF without losing the formatting?</b></summary>
<br>

In Snap Translate, drag the PDF file into the documents window or onto the floating button. The file is translated with the same tables, the same images and the same design, even when it's a scanned PDF. You can drag several files, and they're translated in a queue, one after another.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I translate text on my screen or in an image?</b></summary>
<br>

In Snap Translate, press Ctrl+Alt+T, and the translation appears over the original text in any window: a website, an app, a game, an image or a screenshot. There's no need to copy text or switch to another window.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is there translation software that works offline?</b></summary>
<br>

Yes. Snap Translate runs the translation model on your computer, so screen and document translation work even without a connection. You only need the internet to download the model on first use and to check for updates.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Are my screenshots or documents sent to a server?</b></summary>
<br>

No. Snap Translate translates on the computer itself, so it works even without an internet connection. The software connects to the network only on first use, to download the model, and to check for updates. The full details are in the <a href="https://lifeai.ovh/en/privacy">privacy policy</a>.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How many languages does Snap Translate translate?</b></summary>
<br>

Snap Translate translates more than 400 languages, with automatic language detection, even when the same screen shows text in several languages.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which model should I choose?</b></summary>
<br>

In most cases, Snap Translate's default model gives a good balance of quality and speed. For professional texts, choose a larger model or a higher precision, and on modest computers a small model with economy precision.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does the translated document keep its formatting?</b></summary>
<br>

Yes. Snap Translate saves the translated document with the same design, the same tables and the same images, even when it's a scanned PDF.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work with games and images too?</b></summary>
<br>

Yes. Snap Translate translates anything shown on your screen: websites, apps, windowed games, scanned PDFs and images. Even when the screen shows several languages.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does Snap Translate work on a Mac?</b></summary>
<br>

For now, Snap Translate is available only for Windows 11.

</details>
